# Photography

I'm Ayrton Nardiello. I photograph architecture, quiet street scenes, and small details that are easy to miss. I look for strong shapes, considered compositions, and the way light changes a place.

I'm still learning to react to spontaneous moments in the street. This collection is part of that practice: photographs made while travelling and looking closely at the places around me.

## In this book

Currently only photographs from Egypt and the Pinakothek der Moderne in Munich.
